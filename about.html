Tigray Specialization

With a decade-long presence in the field, Tigray Lithos stands as the authority on the geology of the Tigray region. We specialize in mapping complex geological formations unique to the Horn of Africa.

Our expertise extends across metamorphic basement complexes, sedimentary sequences, and volcanic formations prevalent throughout the Ethiopian highlands.

OUR JOURNEY

Company History

2018

Company Founded

Tigray Lithos was established in Mekelle with a small team of passionate geologists committed to serving the region's geological needs.

2019

First Major Project

Completed the comprehensive Danakil Depression Survey, establishing our reputation for precision and reliability in challenging terrains.

2020

ISO Certification

Achieved ISO 9001:2015 certification, demonstrating our commitment to international quality standards in geological consultancy.

2022

Regional Expansion

Expanded operations to cover all four distinct geological zones of Tigray, with partnerships across the Horn of Africa.

2024

Leadership in Hydrogeology

Established dedicated hydrogeology division, supporting sustainable water resource development across rural communities.
